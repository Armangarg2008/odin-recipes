Fluffy Buttermilk Pancakes

Description

Here is the guide to a quick and tasty pancake recipe that will satisfy your taste buds as well as craving of something tasty:

Ingredients

  • 2 cups all-purpose flour
  • 2 tablespoons granulated sugar
  • 2 teaspoons baking powder
  • 1/2 teaspoon baking soda
  • 1/2 teaspoon salt
  • 2 cups buttermilk
  • 2 large eggs
  • 1/4 cup unsalted butter (melted and cooled)

Steps

  1. In a large bowl, whisk together flour, sugar, baking powder, baking soda, and salt.
  2. In a separate bowl, whisk buttermilk, eggs, and melted butter.
  3. Pour the wet ingredients into the dry ingredients and stir gently until just combined (small lumps are normal; do not overmix).
  4. Heat a non-stick skillet or griddle over medium heat and lightly grease with butter or oil.
  5. Pour 1/4 cup batter onto the skillet for each pancake.
  6. Cook until bubbles form on the surface and pop (about 2 to 3 minutes).
  7. Flip carefully and cook the other side until golden brown (about 1 to 2 minutes).
  8. Serve warm with maple syrup and fresh butter.
